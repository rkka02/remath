## 1. Core Soviet & Russian Primary Sources (Math-Net.Ru)

1. **S. L. Sobolev (1954)** — *Об одной новой задаче математической физики* (*On a new problem of mathematical physics*)
   - **Original**: *Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR Ser. Mat.* (Изв. АН СССР. Сер. матем.), **18**:1 (1954), 3–50. (Originates from Sobolev's 1943 wartime work on stability of rotating liquid-filled artillery shells.)
   - **Math-Net.Ru**: `https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/im3488` (`mi.mathnet.ru/im3488`)
   - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Sobolev_1954_IzvAN_18_rus.pdf` (48 pp., OCR text layer intact)
   - **Key Content**: Formulates the non-Kowalevski rotating-fluid PDE system $\partial_t \mathbf{v} + 2\boldsymbol{\Omega}\times\mathbf{v} = -\nabla p$, $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{v}=0$ and its fourth-order scalar reduction $\partial_t^2 \Delta u + \partial_z^2 u = 0$ in bounded domains, proving Hilbert-space well-posedness and continuous dependence on initial data.

2. **R. A. Aleksandryan (1960)** — *Спектральные свойства операторов, порожденных системами дифференциальных уравнений типа С. Л. Соболева* (*Spectral properties of operators generated by systems of differential equations of S. L. Sobolev type*)
   - **Original**: *Trudy Moskov. Mat. Obshch.* (Тр. ММО), **9** (1960), 455–505 (building on his 1949 Moscow State University PhD thesis under S. L. Sobolev).
   - **Math-Net.Ru**: `https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mmo111` (`mi.mathnet.ru/mmo111`)
   - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Aleksandryan_1960_TrMMO_9_rus.pdf` (51 pp., OCR text layer intact)
   - **Key Content**: In the study's notation the Dirichlet streamfunction satisfies $Tu=\lambda^2u$ for $T=\Delta_D^{-1}\partial_z^2$ on $H_0^1(\Omega)$. Its image under $\Delta_D$ satisfies the conjugate problem for $P=\partial_z^2\Delta_D^{-1}$ on $H^{-1}(\Omega)$. Section 4 reduces the streamfunction equation $(1-\lambda^2)u_{zz}-\lambda^2u_{xx}=0$ to a topological circle homeomorphism $b_\lambda = \gamma_\lambda^+ \circ \gamma_\lambda^-$ on $\partial\Omega$ generated by characteristic reflections; §§9 (Theorems 13–14 bis) prove completeness of polynomial eigenfunctions on the disk, and his 1949 dissertation / 1960 paper construct perturbed domains arbitrarily close to the disk exhibiting non-almost-periodic solutions where pure-point structure breaks down.

3. **T. I. Zelenyak (1965)** — *О зависимости от границы решений некоторых смешанных задач для уравнений малых колебаний вращающейся жидкости* (*On the boundary dependence of solutions of some mixed problems for the equations of small oscillations of a rotating fluid*)
   - **Original**: *Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR* (Докл. АН СССР), **164**:6 (1965), 1225–1228 (communicated by Academician S. L. Sobolev, March 22, 1965).
   - **Math-Net.Ru**: `https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/dan31716` (`mi.mathnet.ru/dan31716`)
   - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Zelenyak_1965_DAN_164_rus.pdf` (4 pp.)
   - **Key Content**: Studies boundary dependence of mixed-problem solutions on finite time intervals, citing his own 1961–1962 papers (pp. 1225–1226, refs. 7–8) for the infinite-dimensional integral/almost-periodic subspace and explicitly crediting Aleksandryan's 1949 dissertation (p. 1225, ref. 3) for the construction of smooth domains arbitrarily close to the disk possessing non-almost-periodic solutions. (Expanded in Zelenyak's untranslated 1970 Novosibirsk monograph *Избранные вопросы качественной теории уравнений с частными производными*.)

4. **M. V. Fokin (2001)** — *Гамильтоновы системы в теории малых колебаний вращающейся идеальной жидкости. I* (*Hamiltonian systems in the theory of small oscillations of a rotating ideal fluid. I*)
   - **Original**: *Mat. Trudy* (Матем. тр.), **4**:2 (2001), 155–206; English trans. *Siberian Adv. Math.* **12**:1 (2002), 1–50.
   - **Math-Net.Ru**: `https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mt18` (`mi.mathnet.ru/mt18`)
   - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Fokin_2001_MatTr_4_Part1_rus.pdf` (52 pp.)
   - **Key Content**: Develops the Novosibirsk school's dynamical-systems and spectral analysis (following Fokin's 1974 thesis, 1981 *Sib. Mat. Zh.* **22**:1, and 1994 *Trudy IM SO RAN* **26** singular-spectrum monograph) of small oscillations of an ideal rotating fluid and exact cellular vortex structures.

5. **M. V. Fokin (2002)** — *Гамильтоновы системы в теории малых колебаний вращающейся идеальной жидкости. II* (*Hamiltonian systems in the theory of small oscillations of a rotating ideal fluid. II*)
   - **Original**: *Mat. Trudy* (Матем. тр.), **5**:1 (2002), 167–204.
   - **Math-Net.Ru**: `https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mt106` (`mi.mathnet.ru/mt106`)
   - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Fokin_2002_MatTr_5_Part2_rus.pdf` (38 pp.)

## 2. Modern Microlocal, Fluid-Dynamics & Chess-Billiard Sources (arXiv)

6. **Y. Colin de Verdière & L. Saint-Raymond (2018/2020)** — *Attractors for two dimensional waves with homogeneous Hamiltonians of degree 0*
   - **arXiv**: `arXiv:1801.05582` (*Comm. Pure Appl. Math.* **73**:2, 2020, 421–462).
   - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/ColinDeVerdiere_SaintRaymond_2018_Attractors_1801.05582.pdf`

7. **S. Dyatlov & M. Zworski (2018/2019)** — *Microlocal analysis of forced waves*
   - **arXiv**: `arXiv:1806.00809` (*Pure Appl. Anal.* **1**:3, 2019, 359–384).
   - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Dyatlov_Zworski_2018_ForcedWaves_1806.00809.pdf`

8. **A. Nogueira & S. Troubetzkoy (2020)** — *Chess Billiards*
   - **arXiv**: `arXiv:2007.14773`.
   - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Nogueira_Troubetzkoy_2020_ChessBilliards_2007.14773.pdf`
   - **Key Content**: Formalizes the chess billiard map as an orientation-preserving circle homeomorphism; analyzes circles, triangles (period 1, 2, or 3), general polygons ($\le 3k-4$ periodic cylinders), and arbitrary direction pairs $(\theta_1,\theta_2)$ on the square (periodic direction pairs contain an open dense subset, countable 1D curves of neutral orbits).

9. **M. Lenci, C. Bonanno & G. Cristadoro (2021/2023)** — *Internal-wave billiards in trapezoids and similar tables*
   - **arXiv**: `arXiv:2102.01654` (*Nonlinearity* **36**, 2023, 1029–1052).
   - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Lenci_Bonanno_Cristadoro_2021_InternalWaveBilliards_2102.01654.pdf`
   - Proposition 3.1 uses the period of a wall section. An even denominator gives global finite order, and odd denominators support tongues. Theorem 3.2 gives almost-everywhere rational rotation in a two-parameter domain for fixed length. Remark 3.5 and (3.10) give the two break-return equations for tongue boundaries. Appendix A treats fixed dilation and varying translation. The one-parameter conclusion for each fixed physical tank requires an additional argument. [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6544/ac98ef)

10. **S. Dyatlov, J. Wang & M. Zworski (2021/2025)** — *Mathematics of internal waves in a 2D aquarium*
    - **arXiv**: `arXiv:2112.10191` (*Analysis & PDE* **18**, 2025, 1–92).
    - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Dyatlov_Wang_Zworski_2021_Aquarium_2112.10191.pdf`
    - **Key Content**: Establishes a microlocal limiting absorption principle and long-time attractor singularity profile for forced Sobolev/Poincaré internal waves in smooth 2D aquaria under Morse–Smale assumptions.

11. **S. Zhu, under the direction of Z. Li (2022)** — *On the Smoothness and Regularity of the Chess Billiard Flow and the Poincaré Problem*
    - **arXiv**: `arXiv:2210.13384`.
    - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Zhu_Li_2022_ChessBilliard_2210.13384.pdf`
    - Section 4.1 estimates the dimension numerically for fixed tilted squares.

12. **Z. Li (2023)** — *2D internal waves in an ergodic setting*
    - **arXiv**: `arXiv:2301.12365`.
    - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Li_2023_ErgodicInternalWaves_2301.12365.pdf`

13. **Z. Li (2024)** — *Internal waves in aquariums with characteristic corners*
    - **arXiv**: `arXiv:2402.01896`.
    - **Local Archive**: `sources/papers/Li_2024_CharacteristicCorners_2402.01896.pdf`
    - **Key Content**: Extends Dyatlov–Wang–Zworski to piecewise-smooth domains with characteristic corners under Morse–Smale hypotheses, uncovering the corner characteristic ratio $\alpha_\kappa(\lambda)$ and the strict threshold $|\log\alpha_\kappa(\lambda)| < \sqrt{3}\pi$ (Theorem 2 & Remark 2) for corner singularities to remain in $H^1$ away from the attractor.

## Primary literature located during Phase 1

The following works supplement the 13 archived PDFs. The links point to online copies or publisher records.

- Stefan Kopecz (2006), *Fractal internal wave patterns in a tilted square*. Theorem 2 and the discussion following it give the primary square attractor, its focusing product and the saturation tilt. On p. 9 he derives $k(t_1+t_2)=1$ for arbitrary positive $k$. [Author's report](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~maas0131/files/kopecz2006.pdf)
- J. Bajars, J. Frank and L. R. M. Maas (2013), *On the appearance of internal wave attractors due to an initial or parametrically excited disturbance*. Section 2.4 treats periodic and focusing families in a tilted square. [Journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2012.479) · [Author's manuscript](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~frank011/Articles/BaFrMa12.pdf)
- C. R. H. Hanusa and A. V. Mahankali, *A billiards-like dynamical system for attacking chess pieces*. Sections 6–7 include square cycles and questions about rigid cycles under perturbations. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1901.01917)
- S. Troitskaya, work on characteristic wedges and rotating-fluid basins. [1999 paper](https://www.mathnet.ru/links/e355d3624dcb24d144f7c3664ee1c6d7/mzm1052.pdf) · [2017 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06605)
- A. M. M. Manders, J. J. Duistermaat and L. R. M. Maas (2003), *Wave attractors in a smooth convex enclosed geometry*, and G. I. Ogilvie (2005), *Wave attractors and the asymptotic dissipation rate of tidal disturbances*. These treat boundary perturbations and focusing in specific geometries. [Manders et al.](https://www.staff.science.uu.nl/~maas0131/files/mandersDuistermaatMaas03.pdf) · [Ogilvie](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0506450)
- Louis Soares (2021), *Hecke triangle groups, transfer operators and Hausdorff dimension*. The introduction reviews the established bounds $1/2<\delta(w)<1$ for fixed $w>2$. [Published article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00023-021-01117-1)
- Zhang and Temnov (2026), *Spectral properties of the internal wave operator in nonclassical problems*. Compare its Theorem 2.3 with the two explicit counterexamples in the Phase 1 report. [Publisher text](https://journals.rudn.ru/CMFD/article/view/50766)
