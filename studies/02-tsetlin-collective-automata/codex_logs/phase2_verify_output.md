**Thresholds and signs.** With \(p=P,\ q=1-p,\ l=1-\kappa\), I obtain
\[

\[
 F_D-F_L=\frac{2lpq^2(1-lp)}{1-2lpq},\qquad

\]
Both differences are positive for \(1/2<p<1\) and \(0\le\kappa<1\). At \(\kappa=1\), \(F_L=F_B=F_D=p\) and \(F_K=2p/(1+p)\).
