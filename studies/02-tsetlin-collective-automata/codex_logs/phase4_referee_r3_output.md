Two players can switch together from \(DD\) into shallow \(CC\), receive different feedback there, and reach the mixed core \(M\) first. This contradicts the printed \(Q_{D_0M}=0\) for arbitrary feasible \(\kappa_R\). \[
D_2D_2\longrightarrow D_1D_1\longrightarrow C_1C_1
